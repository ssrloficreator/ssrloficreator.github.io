LOFI CREATOR

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 21, 2024

Lofi Creator ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) like your name, email address, or phone number unless you provide it voluntarily (e.g., for feedback).

  • Audio Files: The app processes audio files locally on your device to create lofi remixes. We do not upload your music files to any server.
  • Voice Recording: If you use the "Voice Over" feature, the app uses your microphone to record audio. These recordings are stored only on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob: To show advertisements. They may use device identifiers to personalize ads.
  • Firebase Analytics: To understand how users interact with the app (e.g., which features are used most).
  • Firebase Crashlytics: To receive crash reports and improve app stability.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): To send push notifications about app updates or features.

3. Permissions We Request

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required for the Voice Over feature.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to pick music files from your device for conversion.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to alert you when a conversion is complete or to send app-related updates.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Since Lofi Creator does not create user accounts or store your personal audio files on cloud servers:

  • Audio Files & Recordings: All remixed audio and voice recordings remain on your device and are managed entirely by you.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by clearing the app cache/data in your phone settings or by uninstalling the application.

5. Ad Personalization & Opt-Out

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Enable Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Delete advertising ID depending on your Android version).

6. Data Security

We prioritize your data security. Because primary audio processing takes place locally on your device, your music and recordings remain secure on your phone. For the minimal analytics and performance data collected via Firebase, we rely on industry-standard encryption and security protocols.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
